Detailed Description

Log-message packet.

Note
In contrast to other packets, the log packet is not "groupable", instead, the minor packet type indicates the log level.

◆ timestamp

chrono::timestamp vidre::lognew::packet::timestamp {}

Timestamp.

Note, that the timestamp is referenced to std::chrono::steady_clock, which is a high-resolution, monotonic clock. This means that the timestamp does not necessarily correspond to wall-clock time, but is suitable for measuring time intervals and ordering log messages.
