Detailed Description

Timestamps are represented as a combination of seconds and nanoseconds, allowing for high-resolution time measurements. The now() function provides a convenient way to obtain the current time as a timestamp, using a high-resolution, monotonic clock to ensure accuracy and consistency across different platforms.

Note
Timestamps do NOT necessarily represent wall-clock time, but rather a monotonic time point that is useful for measuring intervals and durations.
