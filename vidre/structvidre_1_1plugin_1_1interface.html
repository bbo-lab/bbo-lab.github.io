Detailed Description

Plugin interface.

The "interface" describes the "data flow" capabilities of a plugin, i.e. whether it produces and/or consumes data. This is relevant for distinguishing between "source", "sink" and "pipe" plugins, which can be used in different contexts and have different requirements for their implementation.
