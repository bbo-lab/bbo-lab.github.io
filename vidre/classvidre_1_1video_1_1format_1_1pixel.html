C++-API » Video library » Video formats » Pixel format

#include <vidre/video/format/pixel.hh>

Detailed Description

There are a multitude of pixel formats, and they are often encoded in a compact way. This class provides a unified interface to encode and decode pixel formats, and to convert between them.
